📖 Book Name: The Alchemist

👤 Author Name: Paulo Coelho

📅 Published Year: 1988

🏷️ Genre: Fiction / Adventure

About the Book

The Alchemist is a beautiful story about a young shepherd who travels across the world in search of a treasure. The journey teaches him about dreams, courage, purpose, and the true meaning of life.

Why I Like This Book

I like this book because it teaches us to follow our dreams and never give up. The simple language and meaningful message make it an inspiring and enjoyable read.
